Under the hood

Engineering

About 118,000 lines of Luau in 231 modules with Rojo. Server-authoritative gameplay, session-locked saves, 20 automated test suites.

Procedural art

Every building, hub and landmark is generated by headless Blender Python (124 scripts, about 52,000 lines) and pre-fractured for destruction.

Game feel

Anime impact frames, hit-stop, cinematic special-punch cutscenes and an opening cutscene.

Performance

Quality tiers and an fps governor aimed at 50 fps on phones and 100 on PC. An in-game benchmark: an iPhone 12 holds about 60 fps. Join download cut from 35 MB to 1.6 MB.

Live ops

02 Unity 6 · URP / In development

Handheld Combat

A first-person 1v1 sword-dueling game for Windows PC where your iPhone is the motion controller. A Safari page streams the phone's gyro at 60 Hz over WebRTC, paired by QR code.

Status a prototype in development, planned as a Steam title. Not released yet.

  • Protocol A versioned binary pose protocol with matching C# and TypeScript codecs, checked by golden vectors in CI.
  • Latency A jitter buffer with gyro extrapolation and a One Euro filter. Measured 60 Hz, p95 26 ms between packets on Wi-Fi.
  • Sword and arm A custom 7-DoF arm IK solver and deterministic blade-clash physics.
  • Body A procedural body with foot planning, foot IK and a runtime ragdoll that gets back up.
  • Tracking Webcam body tracking with BlazePose running in-engine.
  • Networking FishNet + Steamworks, at an early stage: so far only tested on one PC.
  • Scale About 76,000 lines of C# in 268 files, of which about 24,000 lines are tests (900+ tests). Also developed with AI coding agents.
